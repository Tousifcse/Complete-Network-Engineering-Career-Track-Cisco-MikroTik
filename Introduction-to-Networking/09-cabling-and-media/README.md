# 09. Cabling & Transmission Media

Transmission media is the physical or wireless path through which data travels from sender to receiver.

## Guided (Wired) Media

### 1. Twisted Pair Cable
Two insulated copper wires twisted together to reduce electromagnetic interference.
- **UTP** (Unshielded Twisted Pair) – cheaper, commonly used in LANs
- **STP** (Shielded Twisted Pair) – shielded, better noise resistance

### 2. Coaxial Cable
A single copper conductor surrounded by insulation and shielding.
- Used in older cable TV and broadband connections
- Better shielding than twisted pair, but bulkier

### 3. Fiber Optic Cable
Uses light signals through glass/plastic fibers to transmit data.
- ✅ Extremely high speed, long distance, immune to electromagnetic interference
- ❌ Expensive, fragile, harder to install

## Comparison Table

| Media | Speed | Distance | Cost | Interference Resistance |
|-------|-------|----------|------|--------------------------|
| Twisted Pair (UTP) | Moderate | Short | Low | Low |
| Coaxial | Moderate-High | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Fiber Optic | Very High | Long | High | Very High |

## Unguided (Wireless) Media

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Radio Waves | Omnidirectional, can penetrate walls | Wi-Fi, FM radio |
| Microwaves | Line-of-sight, directional | Satellite communication |
| Infrared | Short range, cannot penetrate walls | TV remote controls |

## Connector Types (Common)
- **RJ-45** – used for Ethernet/twisted pair cables
- **BNC** – used for coaxial cables
- **LC/SC** – used for fiber optic cables
